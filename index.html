💡 First-Time Guide: Differentiating Brute Force from Password Spraying
1 Brute Force: 1 single account targeted with 50+ passwords (high lockout risk).
2 Password Spraying: 1 common password tried across 50+ distinct users (`dc(TargetUserName) >= 5`) to evade single-user lockouts.
